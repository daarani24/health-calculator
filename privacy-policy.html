Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Health Calculator Suite handles information when you visit and use this website.

Calculator information

The values you enter into the calculators, such as height, weight, age, and measurements, are processed in your web browser to calculate results. The calculator tools are designed to perform these calculations locally rather than sending those values to a server operated by this website.

Local browser storage

The BMI calculator may use your browser's local storage to remember your most recent BMI result on your device. This is stored by your browser and is not a user account or a server-side health record.

Third-party resources

The website may load third-party resources such as Google Fonts, Tailwind CSS, and the Lucide icon library. Your browser may make requests to those services when the resources are loaded.

Advertising and Third-Party Networks

We use Adsterra, a third-party advertising network, to serve advertisements when you visit our website. Adsterra and its partners may collect and use certain non-personal information about your visits to this and other websites (such as your IP address, device type, geographic region, and browsing behavior) to provide advertisements about goods and services that may interest you.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The website itself is designed to minimize unnecessary tracking. However, third-party services, including our advertising partner Adsterra, may use cookies, web beacons, or unique device identifiers to personalize ads, track ad performance, and prevent fraud. You can manage or disable cookies directly through your individual web browser settings.

Children

This website is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children.

Changes to this policy

I may update this Privacy Policy when the website, services, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page.
