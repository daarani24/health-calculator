How ideal body weight is calculated

This calculator uses the Devine formula. It starts from a base weight of 50 kg for men or 45.5 kg for women and adds 2.3 kg for every inch of height above 5 feet (152.4 cm). The formula was originally developed in 1974 to help estimate medication doses, and it is still used as a simple reference point.

Why different formulas give different answers

Other formulas, such as Robinson, Miller, and Hamwi, produce slightly different numbers because they were built from different data. None of them knows your frame size, muscle mass, age, or ethnicity, so treat the result as a ballpark reference and not as a target you must hit.

Ideal weight versus healthy weight

A single number is rarely the whole story. Health professionals usually look at a healthy range rather than one figure. Use the BMI calculator to see which range your weight falls in, and the body fat calculator for a view of body composition.

Frequently asked questions

Is ideal weight the same as healthy weight?

No. Ideal weight is a single reference figure from a formula, while a healthy weight is a range that depends on your body and health.

Why does gender change the result?

The formula uses a lower base weight for women because, on average, women have a smaller frame and a different body composition than men.

Can I use this for children?

No. This calculator is meant for adults. Children and teenagers should be assessed with growth charts by a healthcare professional.
