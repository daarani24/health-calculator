Daily Water Intake Calculator

Find out roughly how much water you should drink each day.

How much water do you need?

A common starting point is about 35 mL of fluid per kilogram of body weight per day. This calculator adds extra fluid for activity: 5 mL per kg for light activity, 10 mL per kg for moderate activity, and 15 mL per kg for an active lifestyle. For example, a 60 kg person with moderate activity would get about (35 + 10) × 60 = 2,700 mL, or roughly 2.7 liters per day.

What else affects your needs?

  • Hot or humid weather and high altitude increase fluid needs.
  • Illness with fever, vomiting, or diarrhea increases fluid loss.
  • Pregnancy and breastfeeding raise requirements.
  • Some conditions, such as kidney or heart problems, may require limiting fluids, so follow your doctor's advice.

Tips for staying hydrated

Water, tea, milk, and the water in fruits and vegetables all contribute to your daily fluid intake. Sip steadily through the day instead of drinking large amounts at once, and drink more around exercise. Pale yellow urine is a simple everyday sign that you are well hydrated. You can also check your overall numbers with the BMI calculator or the BMR and calorie calculator.

Frequently asked questions

Do coffee, tea, and food count toward my water intake?

Yes. Beverages and the water found in food contribute to your total fluid intake, so you do not need to get every liter from plain water.

What are signs that I am not drinking enough?

Common signs include thirst, dark yellow urine, headache, dry mouth, and tiredness. Severe or persistent symptoms need medical attention.

Can you drink too much water?

Yes, although it is uncommon. Drinking very large amounts in a short time can dilute sodium levels in the blood. Spread your intake through the day.
