How the U.S. Navy method works

This calculator estimates body fat from tape measurements instead of skinfold calipers or scans. It uses your height, neck, and waist, plus your hip measurement for women, in a logarithmic formula developed for the U.S. military. It is popular because it needs only a soft measuring tape.

How to measure correctly

  • Neck: measure just below the larynx (Adam's apple), keeping the tape level.
  • Waist: for men, measure at the navel. For women, measure at the narrowest point of the waist.
  • Hips (women): measure at the widest point of the hips and buttocks.
  • Stand relaxed, do not pull the tape tight, and measure twice to confirm.

How accurate is it?

It is a general estimate and can differ from clinical methods such as DEXA scans by several percentage points. It is most useful for tracking your own changes over time using the same measuring technique. For a quick screening number, you can also check your BMI.

Frequently asked questions

Why must the waist be larger than the neck for men?

The formula uses the difference between waist and neck. If the neck is as large as the waist, the calculation cannot produce a meaningful value, so recheck your measurements.

Why is a hip measurement needed for women?

The formula for women includes hip size because fat distribution differs between men and women.

What is a healthy body fat percentage?

It varies with age, sex, and fitness level. A healthcare professional or qualified trainer can tell you what range suits you.
