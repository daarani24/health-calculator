About Health Calculator Suite

Health Calculator Suite is an independent website created and maintained by a solo developer. The goal is to provide free, simple, and easy-to-use health calculation tools for general informational and educational purposes.

Available calculators

  • BMI Calculator
  • BMR & Daily Calorie Calculator
  • Ideal Body Weight Calculator
  • Body Fat Percentage Estimator
  • Water Intake Calculator

How the calculators work

The tools use established mathematical formulas to produce estimates from information entered by the visitor. Results can vary from person to person and should not be treated as a diagnosis, treatment plan, or substitute for professional medical advice.

Independent project

This is a small independent project, not a hospital, clinic, medical practice, or healthcare organization. I maintain the website and may improve or add calculators over time.
